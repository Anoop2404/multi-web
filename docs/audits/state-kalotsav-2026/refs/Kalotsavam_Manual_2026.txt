Confederation of Kerala Sahodaya Complexes

The Confederation of Kerala Sahodaya Complexes is a collective forum of CBSE schools that
believes education is not a race to the top but a path we walk together. The word Sahodaya means
‘rising together,’ and this idea shapes the identity and purpose of the Confederation. It stands as a
reminder that progress in education is not about individual achievement but about shared
growth.Teachers supporting teachers, schools supporting schools, and children finding confidence
through the opportunities they share.

Across Kerala, each Sahodaya works as a local cluster of CBSE schools. Within these clusters,
schools exchange resources, encourage professional development, and collaborate on both
academic and cultural activities. The Confederation brings all these clusters under one umbrella,
giving them strength through unity and direction through common purpose. What emerges is an
administrative network and a living community of practice where ideas flow freely and every school
has a voice.

One of the most cherished contributions of the Confederation of Kerala Sahodaya Complexes is the
Keralam State Sahodaya Kalotsavamam. Over the years, this cultural and talent festival has grown
into one of the largest of its kind in the country. To the thousands of students who prepare and
participate, it is a competition and a celebration of creativity, discipline, and diversity. Here, young
people discover their voice, their talent, and the joy of belonging to something larger than
themselves. Teachers too find affirmation in watching their students shine on such a stage, and
                                                   l
schools witness the spirit of cooperation that transcends boundaries.

                                         na
Beyond cultural platforms, the Confederation plays a guiding role in educational transformation. It
works to align schools with the goals of the Central Board of Secondary Education and the National
                                 Fi
Education Policy, ensuring that classrooms remain forward-looking and inclusive. Through
seminars, training programmes, and shared forums, it encourages innovation in pedagogy,
assessment, and technology use. Teachers benefit from continuous learning opportunities, while
schools gain confidence in navigating the challenges of a rapidly changing educational landscape.

What sets the Confederation apart is its ethos. At a time when education can feel fragmented and
competitive, it carries a counter-message.One of collaboration, care, and community. It insists that
excellence need not come at the cost of empathy, and that unity can coexist with diversity. In
bringing together Sahodayas across Kerala, it has created a powerful example of what collective
leadership in education can look like.

The Confederation of Kerala Sahodaya Complexes continues to uphold the values of cooperation
and shared progress, ensuring that CBSE schools in Kerala do not just stand alone but rise
together.




                                                                                                      1
Sahodaya State Kalotsavamam

Sahodaya State Kalotsavamam is an experience that helps students grow as individuals. While
classrooms strengthen knowledge and academics, Kalotsavamam provides a platform where
children can express their creativity, emotions, and imagination. For many students, it becomes the
stage where they discover their talent and gain the confidence to showcase it before others.

In Kerala, the State Government organizes similar cultural festivals for state schools, giving students
from those schools a wide stage to celebrate their gifts. However, CBSE students do not
automatically receive such an opportunity. It is here that the Confederation of Kerala Sahodaya
Complexes plays a vital role. By organizing the CBSE State Kalotsavamam, it ensures that students
from affiliated schools also get a stage equal in scope and spirit to present their talents. Without this
effort, many CBSE students would be left without a common cultural platform of this scale.

The importance of Kalotsavamam lies in the lessons it teaches beyond the competition. Preparing
for events calls for dedication, discipline, and time management. Performing before an audience
develops confidence and courage, while learning to accept both success and failure gracefully
builds resilience. In this way, Kalotsavamam nurtures qualities that last well beyond school years.

The festival also promotes collaboration. Group events bring students together, teaching them to
cooperate, share responsibility, and value teamwork. These experiences enrich friendships and
prepare children for the cooperative world they will face in higher education and professional life.
                                                    l
                                         na
Cultural identity and heritage are equally strengthened. Through music, dance, theatre, and
literature, students connect with tradition while experimenting with creativity. They begin to value
diversity and develop pride in cultural roots, realizing that knowledge is not confined to books but

                                  Fi
also carried in stories, performances, and art forms.

Most importantly, Kalotsavam introduces the idea of healthy competition. Unlike some exams that
measure one kind of success, Kalotsavam celebrates many forms of excellence. Every participant,
whether they win or not, walks away with learning, exposure, and the joy of self-expression.

For CBSE students, the Kalotsavam organized by the Confederation is a festival.It is a bridge that
ensures they are not excluded from a cultural movement that has shaped generations of learners in
the state. It affirms that talent deserves a platform and that every child should have the chance to
rise and be seen.




                                                                                                       2
Importance of the State Kalotsavam Manual

The State Kalotsavam Manual is the foundation on which the entire festival stands. It tells everyone,
students, teachers, organizers, and judges,what to expect and how to move forward. When there is
one clear manual, there is no room for confusion. Every participant knows the rules, every teacher
understands the process, and every judge has a common scale to evaluate.

What makes the manual important is that it is used at every stage. The Kalotsavam begins in
schools, moves to the sahodaya, and finally reaches the state. At the sahodaya level, the same
manual is to be followed without changes. This matters because the students who win at the
sahodaya will step into the state competition next. If the rules or judgement change midway, it
becomes unfair and discouraging. By using one manual throughout, the festival ensures that the
student who performs well at the sahodaya level is fully prepared for what awaits at the state level.

The manual also protects the integrity of judgement. Each competition has clear criteria, with value
points that guide the evaluation. Whether it is a drawing, a song, a dance, or a speech, the same
yardstick applies everywhere. This consistency gives students the confidence that their work will be
judged fairly, no matter where they perform. It also helps judges, who can rely on the manual
instead of personal preference.

For organizers, the manual is a map. It explains how to register participants, how to form
committees, how to handle appeals, and how to manage the smooth flow of events. With so many
                                                  l
schools, categories, and participants, a festival of this scale cannot run without order. The manual

                                        na
gives that order in a way that is practical and dependable.

The manual carries a message of trust. It tells students that their effort will be valued equally,

                                Fi
whether they come from a small school or a large one, from one sahodaya or another. It assures
teachers that the guidance they give to their students will hold good at every level. It also shows
parents that the event is not about chance, but about fairness.

The State Kalotsavam Manual is the shared understanding that keeps the festival honest, united,
and meaningful for everyone involved.

II. General Rules

1.​ One school can represent only one Sahodaya in the State Kalotsavam. Only students from
    schools which are affiliated to CBSE and having membership in a Sahodaya complex shall
    participate in the State Kalotsavam. The list of participants for the Kalotsavam is to be registered
    by the respective Sahodayas.



                                                                                                      9
2.​ Any participant/team from a school can register for State Kalotsavam only through one of the
    registered Sahodayas. Sahodayas can register by sending the following details to the given
    E-Mail ID:​
     a. Name of the Sahodaya​
     b. List of office bearers​
     c. List of member schools​
     d. Contact details

3.​ For registration, a Kalotsavam portal will be provided and details will be informed in advance by
    the organizing committee.

4.​ Passwords for online registration can be obtained by the Sahodaya heads by the date notified
    by the Kalotsavam office.

5.​ The last date of Online registration for State Kalotsavam will be announced each year by the
    organizing committee, along with the final date for corrections.

6.​ One student shall participate in a maximum of five single items and two group items. Out of 5
    single items only 3 can be stage items. However, a student can participate in 5 off-stage items
    provided he or she is not participating in any stage items.

7.​ A student from a lower category cannot compete in a higher category and vice versa.

8.​ An Identity Card issued by the organizing committee of the Kalotsavam is compulsory for
    participating in any event.

                                         na
9.​ Participation in the following common group items shall be irrespective of category or sex: Group
    Song, Patriotic Song, Mime, English One Act Play, Western Music Concert, Band Display.

10.​Participation in the following common group items shall be irrespective of category:

    Thiruvathirakali (Girls), Oppana (Girls), Margham Kali (Girls), Kolkkali (Boys), Duffmutt (Boys).

11.​Even if there is only one entry for an item, the competition shall be conducted and grade, prize
    and points will be awarded.

12.​The Keralam Sahodaya Kalotsavam shall be held at three levels:​
    a. School level​ ​    ​      b. Sahodaya level​     ​       ​           c. State level

13.​Each Sahodaya shall organize Kalotsavam framing rules and regulations in accordance with the
    State Manual.

14.​Only one participant/team from a school can participate in the Sahodaya level competition.

15.​First and second position winners in each item and in each category shall be selected in the
    Sahodaya level to participate in the State Kalotsavam, with a restriction that only two
    participants/teams from a Sahodaya are eligible to register in the State level competition. For the
    English One Act Play (common item) there shall be only one entry from each Sahodaya.

16.​The General Convener of the Sahodaya Kalotsavam shall prepare the list of the winners of the
    first two places in each item. He/she shall ensure that only two entries are made for any event
    and only one team for English One Act Play. It shall be the responsibility of the Sahodaya head
    to register the names of the eligible participants/teams with the State Kalotsavam committee on
    time.

23.​Finance
    The funds for the State Kalotsavam shall be raised through four sources:
    a.​ Contribution from Sahodaya Complexes – Each Sahodaya shall contribute an amount as
        notified at the time of registration for participating in the State Kalotsavam.
    b.​ Individual participation fee shall be as notified at the time of registration, per participant,
        irrespective of the number of events.
    c.​ Sale of food coupons – Participants and escorting teachers shall purchase food coupons
        at the venue, as per the rates fixed by the organizing committee.
    d.​ Sponsorship.

24.​Court Appeals​
    Participants/team failing to get the nomination in the two entries from a Sahodaya and coming
    for the State level competition with an order from a court of law will be allowed to participate in
    their respective event and grades/prizes/awards shall be given to the participant/team. The
    points for grade and prize (if any) will not be added to the school or to the Sahodaya in deciding
    the toppers of the Kalotsavam.

25.​Group items can be registered with two standbys to substitute the performers in case of
    emergency. However, substitution while performing on the stage will not be allowed. Certificates
    and prizes will be given only to those students who perform on the stage. Students registered as
    standby need not pay any registration fee. They will not be issued any Participation Certificate.

28.​In case the prize winners are not participating in the State Kalotsavam, the Sahodaya can

    promotion are to be settled by Sahodaya heads at their level. The State Committee will approve
    only the list forwarded by each Sahodaya head.

29.​Procedures to be followed in participating in an Event

  a.​ Participant/Team Manager shall report at the respective stage at least half an hour before the
      commencement of the competition along with the ID card of the participant(s). Those who fail
      to report at the venue on time may be disqualified without further enquiry. If the number of
      participants is above 20, clusters shall be formed by the organizing committee in advance. If
      there are clusters for the competition, get the time of reporting for that event from the stage
      manager.
  b.​ In case of no clusters or if a participant reaches the venue at the allotted time, the participant
      should surrender the ID card, get the order number through a lot and the chest number, and
      wait for their chance to perform.
  c.​ Perform on the stage as and when the chest number is called out.
  d.​ Participants should return the chest number to the concerned Stage Manager after the
      performance and should collect their ID cards before leaving the venue.

                              INFORMATION TO THE JUDGES

1.​ A separate room is arranged for the judges when they are not involved in the judgement.​

2.​ In no case there shall be any public contact. Even talking to the people in the premises is to
    be avoided.​

3.​ Judgment of an item in a particular category will take place at a stretch inclusive of all
    clusters.​

4.​ There is no separate lunch break. As and when one particular category.​

5.​ If the competition is over, the judges shall be escorted to the mess hall for the lunch by the
    judges-in-charge. If the item prolongs, tiffin will be served at the Judgment seat which they
    consume when a particular cluster in a category gets over.​

6.​ After the judgment the tabulation work will be done by the tabulators in the presence of the
    judges and one of the judges will announce the result on the stage.​

7.​ There will be three lights on the stage: Red, Green and Yellow. Green indicates the starting
    of the item. Yellow indicates there is only one minute left. Red indicates the time limit is over.
    If a participant exceeds the time limit, the person is not eligible for any prize. In the stage
                                                l
    where the light indicator is not provided, the bell system will be allowed.​

8.​ In case a technical fault occurs before half the time of an item, that event will be restarted;

    cluster.​                 Fi
