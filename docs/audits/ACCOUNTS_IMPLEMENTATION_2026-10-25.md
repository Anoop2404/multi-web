# Accounts implementation — 25 October 2026

## Where to find it

Open **Finance hub → Income, expenses & budgets** (or **Accounts ledger → Add income / expense**).

The accounts workspace has Vouchers, New voucher, Vendors, Budgets, Bank accounts and Year closing sections. Vendor bills and financial statements remain separate linked screens.

## Implemented

- Fixed all seven reproduced accounting/access defects from the audit: net reversals, opening-balance double counting, filtered carry-in balances, payable cancellation reversal, payment state validation, balanced-journal enforcement and bank ownership validation.
- Financial-year records now drive ledger year selectors and opening dates. Posting dates must belong to their financial year; closed years reject postings and opening-balance deletion. Existing report month grouping supports SQLite as well as MySQL/Postgres.
- New expense/income vouchers: category, date, vendor, event, region, payment reference, bank/cash selection, private PDF/image bill uploads, and descriptions. Saving does not post money; approval does.
- Pending/posted/rejected/reversed voucher register with date, type, year, vendor, event and region filters. Approval/rejection records the reviewer; reversals require a reason and retain the original journal. Retry-safe submissions and repeated approval/reversal guards prevent duplicate journals.
- Vendor master and voucher-history filter. Existing vendor bills now have partial-payment amount/date/bank/reference forms, payment history, year filters and pagination. Bills/payments generated by the UI have retry keys. Unpaid cancellation reverses only the remaining obligation. Old bills carried forward can be paid in a later open year.
- Distinct bank asset heads and bank↔bank / cash↔bank transfer vouchers. Existing CASH-BANK history is retained; this change does not guess how to split historic balances across banks.
- CSV bank-statement import: columns `date,description,amount`, ISO dates and signed amounts (deposit positive / withdrawal negative); 2 MB and 5,000-row limits; exact repeat imports ignored; atomic validation; matching suggestions within three days and manual confirmation. Matching checks tenant, bank, direction and amount and disallows reuse of reconciled rows. Clearing reconciliation releases the statement match.
- Advances, expense settlements, unused-fund returns and employee reimbursements through expense vouchers. Settlements/returns cannot exceed the remaining advance; settlements must be reversed before reversing their advance.
- Monthly recurring income/expense templates. **Generate due recurring vouchers** creates pending drafts for review; it never automatically posts payments. One due occurrence is generated per template per click; repeat for a backlog. No cron job is installed.
- Expense budgets by financial year, account and optionally event, with actual and remaining amounts. Event actuals use vouchers allocated to that event; general actuals include all journal movements. Region is retained on vouchers for filtering/cost allocation.
- Financial-year closing locks the Sahodaya's year and optionally carries asset/liability balances into an empty later year. Pending vouchers and unbalanced journals block closure. Original postings are retained. Target years with existing transactions require reviewed manual opening balances instead of overwriting them.
- Financial reports: net income/expenses, balance sheet, trial balance, cash-and-bank book and general ledger; date/year/account controls; formatted totals; CSV export and browser Print / Save PDF.
- Ledger UX: meaningful cash/bank, income and expense totals; debit/credit equality shown as an integrity measure; date/account filters; full-width register; advanced journals and account management collapsed by default. Finance hub links directly to the new workflows and banking.
- Fee waivers are tenant-checked, reject approved-receipt mutation, cannot exceed the gross amount and replace the previous waiver rather than deducting it repeatedly.
- New workspace is restricted to Sahodaya admin/finance roles (or superadmin); tenant ownership checks also cover document actions and bill downloads.

## Deployment

Deploy the code and frontend build, then run the new tenant migration before opening the new screens:

```sh
php artisan tenants:migrate-sahodayas --force
php artisan optimize:clear
npm run build
```

Migration: `database/migrations/tenant/2026_11_09_000001_create_finance_workspace_tables.php`.

Set up the correct April–March financial years before recording vouchers. After year closing, set the next year current using existing financial-year settings. This implementation does not repair or rewrite production data, assign historic bank balances automatically or close any live year.

## Limits

This provides bookkeeping workflows, not direct bank connectivity or bank transfers. CSV matching requires review; materially different re-exports are not automatically deduplicated as though they were identical imports. Vouchers support a review step, not a configurable multilevel approval matrix or mandatory separation between maker and reviewer. Recurring generation is an explicit administrator action. Budgets monitor allocation; they do not automatically block spending.

Tax-specific handling, purchase-order/procurement workflows and an asset/depreciation register remain optional follow-up modules from the original audit, requiring their own requirements. No claim of tax/compliance certification or live production verification is made.
