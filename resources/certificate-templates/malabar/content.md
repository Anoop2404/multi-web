# Malabar certificate wording

## Merit

This is to certify that {salutation} {recipient_name_upper} of {school_name_upper} has {achievement_line} in {item_title}, under Category {category_short}, at the Malabar Sahodaya District Kalotsav 2026–27, held at {conduct_location} on {event_dates}, organised by the Sahodaya Schools Complex, Malabar Region.

`{achievement_line}` includes “secured”, for example “secured First Prize with A Grade”.
The recipient's school is independent of the certificate phase venue. Merit venue
and conducted dates come from the assigned certificate phase, with event defaults
for unassigned items.

## Participation

This is to certify that {salutation} {recipient_name_upper} of {school_name_upper} participated in the Malabar Sahodaya District Kalotsav 2026–27, held at {venue} on {event_dates}, organised by the Sahodaya Schools Complex, Malabar Region.

{participation_items_box}

Participation remains one certificate per person, with the event's venue and dates
and a list of participated items, categories and grades when available. Merit
certificate phase overrides do not replace those event-level participation details.

## Samples

Sample PDFs use illustrative student, school, venue and date values; they do not
assign any production items or establish actual event venues/dates. The participation
sample includes seven items to check the maximum individually listed content.

The templates preserve the original artwork's proportions, logos, stamp and
signatures. Participation uses an editable text overlay over the printed merit
heading. Long body content may shrink within the configured font-size floor and
ends before the signature area. The body `bottom` setting is an inset from the
bottom of the page: 22 means the content boundary is at 78% of page height.
